# About Me

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first‑year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). My core research fields are natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests
My active research topics cover three primary directions:
1. LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
2. Hallucination in Vision‑Language Models (VLM)
3. LLM truthfulness and Interpretability

## Academic Background
- **2024‑Present**: Ph.D. in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He
- **2020‑June 2024**: B.Eng., Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). Awarded the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship during undergraduate study.

## Research Experience
I have accumulated multi‑site industry and laboratory research internship experience:
1. **February 2025 – Present**: Research Intern, MINIMAX (AI‑technology company)
2. **June 2024 – September 2024**: Research Intern, Tencent WXG, with advisor Zifei Shan
3. **June 2023 – December 2023**: Research Intern, Shanghai AI Lab, with advisor Prof. Yu Cheng
